Privacy Policy

Your sounds stay with you.

VoxLoop works fully offline. We don’t collect, store or share your personal data. This page explains what that means for the VoxLoop app on Android (Google Play) and iOS (App Store).

Effective date: 30 October 2026

In short

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Your recordings and songs are saved only on your device.
  • We never receive, sell or share your data.

Who we are

VoxLoop (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a voice-looping music app developed by Diogo Monte as an independent developer. This policy covers the VoxLoop app distributed through Google Play and the Apple App Store, and this website.

Information the app uses

Microphone and recordings

VoxLoop asks for microphone access so you can record sounds. The microphone is only used while you press Record. Each recording is trimmed, levelled and saved as an audio file in the app’s private storage on your device. Recordings are never uploaded, transmitted or shared with us or anyone else.

You can decline or revoke microphone access at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings → Apps → VoxLoop → Permissions; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone). Without it, VoxLoop can still play songs you already made, but cannot record new sounds.

Songs and settings

Your step patterns, tempo, sound settings (Level, Tune, Echo, Decay) and preferences are stored locally on your device so your song is there next time you open the app. They never leave your device through VoxLoop.

Information we do not collect

VoxLoop does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers, advertising ID, usage statistics or crash reports. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Sharing and selling

Because we don’t collect personal data, we have nothing to share or sell. We do not share data with third parties, data brokers or advertisers.

Device backups

Your device’s operating system may include VoxLoop’s local files in your own device backups (for example Android backup to your Google account, or iCloud / computer backups on iOS), depending on your device settings. Those backups are handled by Google or Apple under their privacy policies and your account settings; VoxLoop has no access to them.

App stores

When you download VoxLoop, Google Play or the Apple App Store may collect information about you (such as your purchase and download history) under their own privacy policies. We may see aggregated, anonymous statistics that the stores provide to all developers, such as download counts. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may process your IP address and basic browser information to deliver the pages, under the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Google Privacy Policy. The website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking of its own.

Data retention and deletion

Your recordings and songs stay on your device until you remove them. You can replace a sound by recording over it, clear a song with New song, or delete everything by clearing the app’s storage (Android) or uninstalling VoxLoop. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device. Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Security

Your data is kept in VoxLoop’s private app storage, which the operating system protects from other apps. Keeping your device locked and up to date is the best way to protect it.

Children

VoxLoop is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Because recordings never leave the device, no child’s voice or data is ever sent to us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/EEA and UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, and to object to its processing. As VoxLoop doesn’t collect or hold personal data, these requests usually have nothing to act on, but you are always welcome to contact us with questions.

Changes to this policy

If VoxLoop ever changes how it handles data, for example by adding an optional feature that needs an internet connection, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change ships, and describe it in the app’s release notes.
