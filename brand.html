Brand

A breath, turned into sound.

VoxLoop is warm, playful and unhurried. It invites people to make noise without making them feel judged. Everything here follows from one idea: your voice is the instrument.

The mark

Two swells and a thread.

A single voice waveform: a big swell, a smaller answer, joined by a thin line that runs through both. It reads as a breath, a word, a loop about to repeat. The mark is the only place in the brand that uses a gradient: Nature Green rising into deep sage.

Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of its smaller swell. Use the one-colour mark on photos, on sage, or wherever a gradient would fight the background. Never stretch, outline, rotate or recolour the gradient.

Type

Soft serif, honest sans.

Fraunces, with its softness turned all the way up, gives headlines a friendly, hand-made warmth. DM Sans keeps everything else clear and calm. The app itself uses the platform fonts, so it feels native on every phone.

Voice

Like a friend who plays, not a teacher who grades.

Short sentences. Plain words. Invitations instead of instructions. When something goes wrong, we say what happened and what to do next, gently.

We say

  • “Sound 1 is waiting for you.”
  • “Changed your mind? Bring it back.”
  • “Tap any sound to hear it.”
  • “Listening… tap stop whenever you’re ready.”

We avoid

  • “Error: recording failed.”
  • “Are you sure? This cannot be undone.”
  • Exclamation marks, streaks and scores.
  • Jargon like “sample rate” or “quantize” in the interface.
The name

Vox, for voice. Loop, for the groove it becomes.

Always written as one word with two capitals: VoxLoop.
